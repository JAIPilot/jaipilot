# Community code of conduct

JAIPilot is built for thoughtful, evidence-driven engineering. We want its community spaces to
reflect the same standard.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and tradeoffs—not people.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce a claim.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and project ownership.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Accept correction gracefully and help turn disagreement into a testable outcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing,
publishing private information, sustained disruption, and bad-faith manipulation of evidence are
not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive community.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect reporters from
retaliation.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.
